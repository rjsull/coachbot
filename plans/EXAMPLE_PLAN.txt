EXAMPLE TRAINING + NUTRITION PLAN
=================================
This is the written plan the shipped example JSON encodes. It is an invented
example, included so the engine runs out of the box. Replace it with your own
written plan and matching JSON.

Baseline
--------
Height 5'10". Starting point is whatever your first check-in records. Weight is
always the weekly mean of morning weigh-ins; one weigh-in is enough, more is
better.

Targets
-------
  Calories, training days ........ 2750
  Calories, off days ............ 2600
  Protein ....................... 180 g
  Training days per week ........ 4
  Off days per week ............. 3
  Training sessions ............ 5 (the fifth is a short conditioning or
                                   skill session, not a full lifting day)

Rate of loss
------------
  0.35 to 0.4 lb per week. Inside the band, hold everything. Outside it for a
  single week, still hold: one week is not a trend.

Landing
-------
  Weight ......................... 166 to 168 lb
  Waist (navel) .................. 32 to 32.5 in
  Ceiling ........................ May 2027
  Early-stop decision band ....... 169 to 171 lb, taken with a completed
                                   portion recheck and a visible waist change

Guardrails
----------
Three biofeedback signals: session energy, sleep quality, and movement quality
on swing work. A signal that moves from fine to worse since the last check-in
raises a recovery guardrail: add 100 to 150 calories and repeat last week's
training. Only a worsening status creates a new increase. A signal that is
already bad and stays bad is not a new event.

Stall protocol
--------------
Three slow or flat weeks in a row:
  Step one  ... Recheck portions honestly, including the ones that are easy to
                round down. Nothing else changes.
  Step two  ... Only after the recheck is marked complete, reduce dinner carbs
                by about one third.

Deload
------
As needed, not scheduled. The engine surfaces stop conditions. It never applies
a deload on its own, and a deload week earns no load increase afterwards.

Progression
-----------
Load climbs only when the week was on pace and no guardrail fired. Blocks run
two weeks. Load is frozen after the freeze date so the landing is not chased
with new strength work.

Medical
-------
This is deterministic plan-tracking software, not medical advice. Any medical
flag in a check-in stops automated recommendations entirely and routes to a
qualified professional.
